🚀 THE PATHWAY

Tournament Progression

How dancers and crews advance from national stages to the world final arena.

PHASE 01

National Qualifiers

13 international host countries run official GLX sanctioning battles and crew divisions. Top 3 podium finishers in each division earn direct advancement tickets.

PHASE 02

Continental Seeding

Qualified crews and battlers receive official delegation badges, international scoring rank, and seeds into the main Bangkok tournament brackets.

PHASE 03

World Finals Bangkok

July 7th – 11th, 2027: 5 monumental days at the MBK Center Bangkok featuring Street Dance, OpenStyle, Country Showcase, and 1v1 / 2v2 / 5v5 World Battles.
